YouTube Shorts Automation

Operated by Suresh Rana.

About this application

YouTube Shorts Automation is a personal content production and publishing tool under development for YouTube channels owned or managed by its operator. It is not a public service.

The planned system prepares short videos and uploads them to the YouTube channel selected and authorized by the operator.

Google and YouTube access

The application requests YouTube read-only access to identify and check the authorized channel, and YouTube upload access to upload videos with their titles and descriptions. Access requires the Google account holder's authorization.

You can review or revoke access through Google Account connections .
